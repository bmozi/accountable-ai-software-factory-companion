# Enterprise Agent Role and Decision-Rights Map

## Purpose

Design an enterprise opportunity system whose agents enlarge research and
judgment without granting themselves authority over values, capital, risk, or
consequence.

## Role map

| Agent perspective | May produce | Must expose | May not decide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Market research | trends, competitor evidence, dated signals | sources, freshness, uncertainty | strategy or legality |
| Customer research | synthesized needs and contradictions | sample, consent, excluded voices | whose interests prevail |
| Product | opportunity briefs and experiments | alternatives, assumptions, harms | portfolio admission |
| Marketing | positioning and demand tests | audience, claims, manipulation risk | acceptable customer treatment |
| Finance | scenarios and lifecycle estimates | ranges, assumptions, tail costs | capital commitment |
| Architecture | options and tradeoffs | constraints, failure modes, reversibility | residual-risk acceptance |
| Security/privacy/legal | findings and required controls | scope, confidence, unresolved issues | business consequence alone |
| Engineering | plans and candidates | provenance, assumptions, evidence claims | release authority by default |
| Evidence | tests and verdicts within scope | methods, artifact binding, limitations | organizational value |
| Operations | readiness and recovery evidence | capacity, modes, ownership gaps | product desirability |
| Outcome | observed effects and countermetrics | windows, denominators, confounders | self-renewal or expansion |
| Portfolio | ranked options and dispositions | displaced work and dissent | final admission unless delegated |

## Common-mode review

Different role names are not independent if they share:

- the same initial framing;
- the same model or provider failure;
- one context bundle;
- incentives optimized for approval or activity;
- circular citations;
- the same synthetic customer assumptions;
- an evaluator created by the producer;
- a missing stakeholder.

For consequential decisions, introduce independence through different evidence,
methods, incentives, or authorized human perspectives—not personas alone.

## Scheduled-agent contract

For recurring research or analysis, record:

- schedule and event trigger;
- approved sources, credentials, and retention;
- freshness requirement;
- cost and volume limits;
- duplicate and contradiction handling;
- prohibited external actions;
- output destination and accountable reader;
- expiry if no one uses the output;
- condition that pauses the schedule.

Scheduled production without a named decision consumer creates unattended
inventory, not organizational intelligence.

## Worked fictional example

Meridian Ledger schedules market and customer agents to identify payment-
reconciliation problems. A product agent prepares three opportunity briefs; a
finance agent supplies ranges; architecture and security agents challenge the
options. All use separate evidence cards, and dissent remains visible. The
portfolio council rejects one proposal, sends one into a thirty-day exploration
envelope, and admits one narrow advisory workflow. No agent can admit its own
proposal or broaden the production authority.
