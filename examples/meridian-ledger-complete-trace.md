# Meridian Ledger: A Complete Accountable Decision Trace

Meridian Ledger and every person, event, quantity, and result below are
fictional instructional composites. The trace demonstrates decisions; it does
not claim measured factory effectiveness.

## 1. Opportunity and boundary

Payment specialists lose time reconciling contradictory source records. The
team proposes an advisory interface that shows the conflict and suggests the
next investigation step. It does not authorize a payment, alter a source
record, or communicate with a customer.

**Decision:** explore one advisory work class using synthetic and approved
de-identified records. Production credentials and writes are prohibited.

## 2. Build, buy, or assemble

The team compares extending the packaged reconciliation product, purchasing an
agent platform, assembling an internal path from existing services, and
building a dedicated system. They score time to evidence, identity and policy
fit, decision-record portability, recovery, exit cost, and continuing
obligation—not feature count alone.

**Decision:** assemble a narrow internal path around replaceable components
while retaining authoritative Work Orders, evidence, dispositions, and
receipts.

## 3. Factory contract

- **Accepts:** one approved advisory reconciliation work class.
- **Produces:** a candidate, criterion-level evidence, disposition, and receipt.
- **May change:** isolated advisory code and tests.
- **May not change:** payment state, source records, customer communication,
  production identity, or evidence policy.
- **Stops on:** missing owner, stale contract, decisive failure, budget
  exhaustion, ambiguous external effect, or boundary escape.

## 4. Work Order

- **Promise:** show a specialist the source conflict and a supported advisory
  recommendation.
- **Consequence owners:** Rina for workflow, Marcus for security exception, Jon
  for release and recovery.
- **Criteria:** contract tests, explanation fidelity, prohibited-write test,
  accessibility, security policy, and recovery drill.
- **Prohibitions:** no source mutation, automated match, customer message, or
  production credential.
- **Budget:** one bounded attempt and one repair attempt.
- **Disposition:** human approval required; no autonomous release.

## 5. Candidate and evidence

The candidate digest is `sha256:fictional-candidate-002`. Contract,
accessibility, and prohibited-write checks pass. The explanation evaluator
passes eight cases and fails two involving reversed dates. A second evaluator
calls both acceptable because the proposed match is correct.

The evidence is coherent enough to disagree. The first evaluator tested the
promise to reveal conflict; the second tested only match correctness.

## 6. Disposition

Rina records **repair**, not approval. Correct matching is not the only claim.
The repair changes the explanation, creates a new candidate digest, and
invalidates all earlier candidate-bound verdicts.

The revised candidate passes the declared evidence floor. Jon authorizes a
read-only canary for five specialists. Passing evidence earns this exposure; it
does not grant authority to automate matches.

## 7. Release receipt

The receipt records:

- intent and factory-contract versions;
- candidate digest and repository reference;
- authority actually used;
- evidence records, dissent, and disposition;
- release actor, time, and five-person exposure;
- semantic operation identity;
- rollback and reconciliation owner; and
- outcome window and countermetrics.

## 8. Interruption and reconciliation

The release adapter sends the exposure request and loses the response. The
attempt becomes indeterminate. Blind retry could create two canary cohorts and
destroy the outcome comparison.

The factory stops, preserves the operation identity, observes provider state,
confirms that the first exposure exists, and completes the receipt without a
second effect.

## 9. Outcome

Specialists reach supported dispositions sooner, but total queue age changes
little because missing source records remain the binding constraint. Review
preparation is higher than expected, and one specialist uses screenshots
outside the intended record.

**Disposition:** effective for a narrow operator condition, insufficient for
the headline queue outcome, and unsafe to expand until evidence capture
improves.

Authority remains limited to the original cohort while the team repairs the
record path. Candidate speed is not reported as business success.

## 10. Learning and retirement test

The team records an observation: reconciliation before retry prevented a
duplicate exposure. It becomes a candidate learning for effectful adapters,
not a universal rule for all tools. A separate test must establish scope,
contradictions, and false-stop cost before it can influence additional work
classes.

At the next review, the team must renew the canary, narrow it, accept a funded
operating obligation, or retire it. Continued existence is not approval.

## Reader challenge

the provider cannot report whether the exposure exists. Identify the required
