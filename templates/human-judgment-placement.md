## Judgment interface

For every retained human decision, specify:

- decision and consequence owned;
- evidence presented and its limitations;
- viable alternatives, including stop;
- affected people and obligations;
- time available and response expectation;
- authority to question, redirect, delay, override, or escalate;
- independent challenge required;
- receipt preserved after the decision.

## Attention recovery

List every review step that only repeats a deterministic check. Remove or
automate those steps before asking humans to absorb more generated work. Record
which consequential question receives the recovered attention.

## Failure test

Present a technically passing candidate whose preferred schedule conflicts with
a customer promise or security obligation. The interface passes only if the
reviewer can identify the affected party, see the conflict, select a real
alternative, and stop the consequence.
