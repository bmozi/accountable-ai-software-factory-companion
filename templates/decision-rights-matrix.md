## Concentration test

Circle any row where the same actor proposes, evaluates, decides, executes, and
owns the reported outcome. For each concentration, record whether the work is
low-consequence enough to accept it or which independent check must be added.

## Courage test

- Who can stop the preferred answer?
- Can that person see the evidence and affected parties?
- Can they act before the consequence becomes irreversible?
- Which incentive makes dissent difficult?
- What protects the challenger from having to rely on personal heroics?
