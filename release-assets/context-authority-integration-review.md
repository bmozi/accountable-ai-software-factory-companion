# Context and authority companion integration review

John Briggs companion repository · 25 November 2026 · unreleased 1.2.1 update.
Scope: public teaching material, reader navigation, executable lesson and bundle.
This is not a new book edition, tagged release or production-platform assessment.

## Reader outcome and compatibility

A reader can follow an architecture question through an interactive responsibility
view, paired chapter, failure exercise and expected evidence. The connected
fictional Meridian Ledger lesson separates corrected guidance, current execution
permission, missing checks and learning admission. The book remains the operating
guide; the laboratory demonstrates selected mechanisms and their limits.

The update extends existing material rather than reproducing a private platform.
Chapter numbers, printed `companion/...` links, the book-only lesson, twenty-four
published obligations, four public schemas, nineteen templates, nine pattern cards
and five canonical Mermaid views remain unchanged. The optional lesson adds twelve
separate tests. The existing teaching factory remains authoritative; no model,
cloud, policy engine, database server or new dependency is required.

The local source-version registry is direct-source and in-memory. Actors are
fixture strings, not authenticated users. A required missing check withholds
canonical disposition and receipt. Candidate learning is admitted by a scripted
owner decision; a fictional insufficient observation is not proof of semantic
truth, transferred benefit or legitimate operational policy.
