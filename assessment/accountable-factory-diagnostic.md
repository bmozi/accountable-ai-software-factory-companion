# Accountable Factory Diagnostic

This diagnostic returns a responsibility profile, not one maturity score. For
one work class, mark each statement:

- `0 — absent or unknown`
- `1 — described but inconsistently practiced`
- `2 — repeatable with evidence`
- `X — not applicable`, with an explanation

## Intent

1. A named outcome and affected stakeholder govern the work.
2. Prohibitions and unresolved decisions are explicit.
3. Exploration cannot become production without translation and admission.

## Authority

4. Identity and permission are bound to the Work Order and work class.
5. Data, tools, environments, budgets, time, and concurrency are bounded.
6. Expansion changes one authority dimension and remains revocable.

## Evidence

7. Required evidence is declared before the result is known.
8. Evidence is bound to the exact candidate and records limitations.
9. Producer claims cannot silently become decisive verdicts.

## Recovery

10. Indeterminate side effects reconcile before retry.
11. Restart preserves authoritative state and prevents duplicate action.
12. Degraded, read-only, drain, stopped, rollback, and retirement paths are
    exercised where relevant.

## Human judgment

13. Each consequential decision has a competent owner with evidence and time.
14. Mechanical checks are removed from scarce human attention where reliable.
15. People can refuse, stop, appeal, override, and record dissent without
    activity incentives punishing them.

## Platform service

16. Users can understand stops, exceptions, support, and service modes.
17. The paved road has a supported exit and does not become a hidden queue.
18. Formation and guardrails progress with demonstrated authority.

## Economics

19. Cost follows work from candidate through supportable outcome.
20. Binding evidence, review, integration, recovery, or demand capacity is
    funded before adding generation.
21. Experiments, operated obligations, liabilities, and retirement are distinct.

## Outcomes

22. Activity, delivery, outcome, and supportability have separate denominators.
23. Every desired measure has a likely harm or burden countermetric.
24. Ineffective, harmful, stopped, and insufficient-evidence dispositions remain
    visible.

## Learning

25. Records, observations, candidate learnings, validated practices, and retired
    guidance have distinct states.
26. Contradiction, scope, provenance, review, decay, and retirement govern
    future influence.
27. Improvement cannot approve broader authority or weaken its own evidence floor.

## Retirement

28. Every operated capability has a named continuing owner and funding source.
29. Dependency, data, user, record, communication, and recovery obligations are
    known.
30. Renewal, consolidation, transfer, and retirement are ordinary portfolio
    dispositions.

## Interpret the result

For each domain, report:

- number of applicable questions;
- number at `2`;
- oldest or highest-consequence `0`;
- evidence supporting every `2`;
- one bounded investment that improves the weakest responsibility; and
- one condition that should stop or narrow the work class now.

Do not average domains into one score. A high mean can hide missing release
authority, unsafe retry, or an unowned consequence. The weakest responsibility
sets the credible operating boundary.
