## Completion test

The companion has done its job when you can show—not merely claim—that:

1. intent and prohibitions were durable before execution;
2. authority was enforced outside model persuasion;
3. evidence was bound to the exact candidate;
4. disagreement received an attributable disposition;
5. indeterminate effects reconciled before retry;
6. release created an owned receipt and recovery path; and
7. observed outcomes could narrow or retire authority.
