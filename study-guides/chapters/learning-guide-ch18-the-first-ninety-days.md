# Learning Guide — Chapter 18: The First Ninety Days

> **Learning Objectives** — After completing this chapter, you will be able to:
>
> 1. Define a bounded ninety-day factory pilot with explicit eligibility and stopping.
> 2. Sequence baseline, draft-only, team-use, and authority decisions.
> 3. Evaluate mixed outcomes using primary measures, countermetrics, and recovery evidence.
> 4. Construct a day-ninety disposition memo that separates capability from learning.

## Study Guide

### Key Terms

- **Pilot:** A bounded test of a capability and its operating model.
- **Eligibility:** The rule deciding which work may enter the pilot.
- **Draft-only:** A phase in which outputs cannot create production consequence.
- **Observation window:** The period during which outcomes and harms are measured.
- **Day-ninety memo:** The evidence-backed decision to preserve, expand, revise, narrow, or stop.

### Review Questions

1. Why should the first pilot be both useful and limited?
2. What happens in each of the four pilot phases?
3. How can a failed pilot create value?
4. Which two decisions must remain separate at day ninety?

### Discussion Questions

1. What work class in your organization is consequential enough to teach but reversible enough to pilot?
2. Under what result should authority be reduced even when the primary metric improves?

### Exercises

**Exercise 18.1 (Core): Run the paper pilot.** Walk eligible and ineligible tasks through every phase.
*Deliverable:* A completed ninety-day pilot workbook.
*Assessment:* Entry, stop, owner, evidence, recovery, and expansion rules are executable.

**Exercise 18.2 (Core): Resolve a mixed outcome.** Assume technical success and primary-metric improvement alongside countermetric harm.
*Deliverable:* A disposition record.
*Assessment:* Repair, rollback, capability authority, and future learning influence are decided separately.

**Exercise 18.3 (Challenge): Write the day-ninety memo.** Synthesize baseline, evidence, limitations, costs, human effects, and recommendation.
*Deliverable:* A decision-ready day-ninety memo.
*Assessment:* The memo supports preserve, expand, revise, narrow, or stop without relying on activity theater.
